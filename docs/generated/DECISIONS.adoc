[[DEC-TIER-PRODUCT]]
== DEC-TIER-PRODUCT: The family model is held to the product life-cycle tier

Status:: proposed
Date:: 2026-11-07

=== Context

The family model composes the core and the SDS1000CML+, both held to the product tier. Its own requirements and requirement links are proposed and claimed until the family owner reviews them, so the model declares no life-cycle tier yet and is checked with the engine's untiered severities.

=== Decision

Once the family owner approves the family requirements and accepts their requirement links, engmod.yml declares the product tier with this decision as its tier decision.
Tier: Product
Bundles: Foundation

=== Consequences

- A scope model composed by the family must be at the product tier or carry an accepted below-tier risk; the DHO1000 joins when it is public and its model reaches the product tier, as the SDS800X HD did.
- The family's architecture decisions stay in the core's decision log (open-dso-core/model/decisions.yml); this log holds only decisions about the family model itself.
